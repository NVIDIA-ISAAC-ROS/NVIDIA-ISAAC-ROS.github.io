Teleoperate the Unitree G1
==========================

This tutorial will guide you on how to teleoperate the Unitree G1 via an XR headset.

.. figure:: :ir_lfs:`<resources/isaac_ros_docs/repositories_and_packages/isaac_ros_physical_ai/isaac_ros_unitree_g1_teleop_bringup/unitree-g1-teleop-hero.gif>`
   :width: 520px
   :height: 293px
   :align: center
   :alt: Unitree G1 teleoperation with an XR headset

Run this tutorial on a Jetson AGX Thor connected to the G1, either as an
external system or as a Thor backpack.

Follow the steps in **Tutorial: Unitree G1 XR Teleop** in
:doc:`/repositories_and_packages/isaac_ros_physical_ai/isaac_ros_unitree_g1_teleop_bringup/index`.

=======================
Configure Your Robot
=======================

First review :doc:`the product requirements </getting_started/requirements>`.
Choose the robot setup used by your manipulation workflow. The shared robot
pages retain driver, gripper, and hardware instructions from Isaac ROS.
Read their safety guidance before connecting to or operating a physical robot.

IsaacLab Training
=================

Reusable downstream Isaac Lab training repository for custom task formulations,
shared MDP terms, and policy export workflows.

.. note::

   To train a simulation policy for your specific robot, first generate a calibrated robot USD
   from its kinematics, as described in
   :ir_docs:`Generate a Calibrated USD for Isaac Lab Training <robots/flexiv/index.html#generate-a-calibrated-usd-for-isaac-lab-training>`.
   Then pass it to training with ``env.scene.robot.spawn.usd_path``.

Get Started
-----------

#. :doc:`setup_training`: install the training package with Isaac Sim and run a short training test.
#. :doc:`tasks/displayport_insertion`: train the DisplayPort insertion policy, export it with LEAPP,
   and deploy it with Isaac ROS. It also lists :ref:`training times and recommended settings
   <training-time-and-recommended-settings>`.

Trainable Policies
------------------

This repository currently packages DisplayPort cable insertion policies for the
Flexiv Rizon 4s arm with a Grav parallel gripper.

.. list-table::
   :widths: 35 45 20
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Policy
     - Task ID
     - Notes
   * - DisplayPort task-space insertion (PhysX)
     - ``IsaacTraining-DisplayPortInsertion-Rizon4s-TaskSpace-ROS-Inference``
     - Recommended. More stable training and the best results in real-robot deployment.
   * - DisplayPort task-space insertion (Newton)
     - ``IsaacTraining-DisplayPortInsertion-Rizon4s-TaskSpace-Newton-ROS-Inference``
     - Experimental. Newton 1.6 MJWarp profile; nominal by default with a calibrated-USD override.

.. note::

   PhysX training is more stable, while Newton training is still experimental. We recommend the PhysX-trained
   policy for the best results in real-robot deployment.

For visualization and evaluation, play a training task id directly: the play scripts apply the
configuration's :meth:`play_mode` overrides (fewer environments, wider spacing, observation
corruption disabled). Pass ``--train_env_cfg`` to play the training configuration unchanged.
Use the plain non-ROS-inference tasks for ablations you do not intend to deploy directly.

Repository Architecture
-----------------------

The repository separates reusable Isaac Lab infrastructure from concrete task
formulations.

.. list-table::
   :widths: 35 65
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Path
     - Purpose
   * - ``src/isaaclab_training/mdp/``
     - Shared actions, observations, rewards, events, terminations, and noise models.
   * - ``src/isaaclab_training/tasks/``
     - Registered task formulations. DisplayPort insertion is the first task.
   * - ``src/isaaclab_training/cli/``
     - Policy export and policy I/O validation commands.
   * - ``src/isaaclab_training/export/``
     - LEAPP export helpers used by the generic exporter.
   * - ``tests/``
     - Lightweight checks for task registration and export-specific tensor semantics.

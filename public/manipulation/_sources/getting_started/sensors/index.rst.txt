==========================
Cameras for Manipulation
==========================

The DisplayPort perception configuration uses one RealSense D455 camera.
For details, refer to :doc:`the product requirements <../requirements>`.
The real-robot manipulation journey links to RealSense setup and manipulation
camera calibration below. Use the camera models, placement, and calibration
requirements specified by your chosen workflow. This page does not extend the
supported sensor list.

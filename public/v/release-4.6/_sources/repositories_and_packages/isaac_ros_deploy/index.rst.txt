Start Here
----------

The Isaac ROS Deploy documentation is organized into four modes. Pick by what
you need:

- :doc:`Tutorials <tutorials/index>`: **learn** by deploying the AGILE
  whole-body policy on a Unitree G1, end to end.
- :doc:`How-to guides <how_to_guides/index>`: **accomplish a task**: bring your
  own policy, bring your own embodiment, or add a custom converter.
- :doc:`Concepts <concepts/index>`: **understand** how LEAPP, runtimes,
  converters, and safety controllers work.
- :doc:`Reference <reference/index>`: **look up** package, controller, and
  converter catalogs.

Related Use Cases
-----------------

- :doc:`AGILE WBC <tutorials/deploy_agile>` - deploy the AGILE whole-body
  control policy on the Unitree G1.

- :doc:`Unitree G1 Isaac Teleop </repositories_and_packages/isaac_ros_physical_ai/isaac_ros_unitree_g1_teleop_bringup/index>` -
  combines IK and learning-based control for whole-body teleoperation.

- :doc:`Unitree G1 GR00T </repositories_and_packages/isaac_ros_physical_ai/isaac_ros_unitree_g1_gr00t/index>` -
  combines the ros2_control and ROS 2 Nodes runtimes for a fine-tuned GR00T
  policy.

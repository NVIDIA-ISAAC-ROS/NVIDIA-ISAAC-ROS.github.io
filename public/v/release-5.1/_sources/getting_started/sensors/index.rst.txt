Sensors Setup
=============

Isaac ROS officially supports RealSense, ZED, Eagle VB1940 (CoE), Hawk (GMSL),
and Sensing GMSL2 cameras.

.. note::

   Argus has not been tested on Jetson AGX Thor and is not supported at this time.


Configure one or more of the cameras:

  * :doc:`realsense_setup`
  * :doc:`zed_setup`
  * :doc:`eagle_setup`
  * :doc:`hawk_setup`
  * :doc:`sensing_thor_setup`


Before using your own camera, calibrate it and configure its driver to publish
accurate ``CameraInfo`` messages. For cameras mounted on robots, see the
extrinsic calibration guides:

   * :doc:`amr_extrinsic_calibration`

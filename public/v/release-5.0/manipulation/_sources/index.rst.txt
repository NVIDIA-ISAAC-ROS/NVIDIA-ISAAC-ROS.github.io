================================================================================
DisplayPort Insertion with Isaac ROS
================================================================================

Train a DisplayPort insertion policy in Isaac Lab, validate it with
hardware-in-the-loop simulation testing, then deploy it to a real robot.

.. note::

   **Required before simulation or real-robot deployment:** Complete
   :doc:`Workspace and Developer Environment Setup <getting_started/environment>`
   to create your source workspace and initialize the Isaac ROS development
   environment. Both tutorials use this shared setup.

      :img-alt: DisplayPort pickup and insertion in Isaac Sim.
